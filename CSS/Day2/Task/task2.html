IT Company Details


ZOHO

Zoho Corporation is a prominent, global technology company founded in 1996 by Sridhar Vembu, Tony Thomas, and Shailesh Kumar Davey, known primarily for its extensive cloud-based business software suite. Headquartered in Chennai, India, with its corporate US headquarters in Austin, Texas, the company operates as a unique, completely bootstrapped entity, meaning it has never taken external venture capital or public funding. Over the years, Zoho has grown into a highly profitable enterprise, surpassing over 100 million global users and generating revenues that cross ₹13,544 crore ($1.5 billion) annually while employing more than 18,000 people worldwide. Their product ecosystem features more than 55 integrated applications, most notably Zoho CRM, Zoho Mail, and the Zoho One operating system, which provide comprehensive tools for sales, marketing, accounting, and human resource management. What truly sets Zoho apart in the tech industry is its fierce commitment to data privacy, as it completely rejects an ad-revenue model to protect customer details, alongside its strong social focus on building software development centers in rural areas to nurture local, non-traditional engineering talent.


TCS

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is a leading multinational information technology services, consulting, and business solutions organization headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra. As the flagship subsidiary of the prestigious Tata Group, TCS has been partnering with many of the world's largest businesses in their digital transformation journeys for over 50 years. The company operates a massive global footprint across dozens of countries, utilizing its unique Global Network Delivery Model to offer a comprehensive portfolio of services including software development, business process outsourcing, cloud infrastructure, and artificial intelligence solutions. With a workforce exceeding 580,000 diverse associates globally, TCS stands as one of the most valuable IT services brands in the world and was the first Indian IT firm to surpass a $100 billion market capitalization.


INFOSYS

Infosys Limited is a premier global consulting and information technology services corporation headquartered in Bengaluru, India. Established in 1981 by seven enterprising engineers, including prominent industry figure N. R. Narayana Murthy, the company originally started in Pune with a meager capital investment of just $250 before shifting base to help pioneer India’s emergence as a technological superpower. Today, it has scaled into a massive multi-national giant generating over $20 billion in annual revenue and employing a vast workforce of more than 328,000 professionals worldwide. Under the core executive guidance of leaders like CEO Salil Parekh and newly appointed CEO-designate Ashiss Kumar Dash, the firm specializes in delivering next-generation digital services, cloud computing architectures, artificial intelligence solutions, and business outsourcing strategies across dozens of countries. Over the decades, the corporation has earned deep industry respect for its rigorous corporate governance models, landmark initial public offering in 1993, and its expansive, state-of-the-art campus infrastructures that prioritize intensive employee upskilling and corporate social responsibility.
