The Spark of the City


Maya always felt invisible in the bustling streets of Chennai, a city pulsing with life from the vibrant Marina Beach to the busy local lanes. But everything changed on a stormy Tuesday evening when a freak lightning strike at the local transformer imbued her with the power to manipulate pure, kinetic energy.
She didn't get a cape or a mask, but she realized she could channel this glowing, electrical current through her fingertips to repair broken streetlights, deflect speeding vehicles, or even sprint across the Adyar River in the blink of an eye. Embracing the ethos of "great power requires great responsibility" often associated with comic legends like Spider-Man, Maya became the city’s silent guardian.
