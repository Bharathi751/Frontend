About College

Established in 2006, Alpha College of Engineering is an AICTE-approved and Anna University-affiliated private institution located on a 60-acre campus in Thirumazhisai, Chennai. It offers UG/PG programs in Engineering and Management, with the highest placement package reaching up to ₹9 LPA.
