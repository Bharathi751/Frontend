Spider-Man and Gwen Stacy


Peter Parker meets Gwen Stacy while studying at Empire State University. Gwen is intelligent, confident, and caring. At first, they don't get along very well, but as they spend more time together, they fall deeply in love. Gwen becomes Peter's first true love and supports him through many difficult moments. Meanwhile, Peter secretly lives a double life as Spider-Man. To protect Gwen, he hides his identity from her. This secret often causes misunderstandings, as Peter frequently disappears to fight criminals and save innocent people. Gwen's father, Captain George Stacy, is a respected police officer. During a battle between Spider-Man and a villain, Captain Stacy is fatally injured while saving a child. Before he dies, he learns Peter is Spider-Man and asks him to protect Gwen. Peter feels responsible for her safety and struggles with guilt. Their relationship continues, but Spider-Man's greatest enemy, the Green Goblin, discovers Peter's secret life. Wanting revenge, the Green Goblin kidnaps Gwen and takes her to the top of the George Washington Bridge (in the original comics). During the rescue, the Green Goblin throws Gwen from the bridge. Spider-Man fires a web and catches her before she hits the water. However, the sudden stop causes a fatal neck injury. Although Peter saves her from falling, he cannot save her life. Gwen's death becomes one of the most heartbreaking moments in Spider-Man's history. Peter is devastated and blames himself for not protecting the person he loved most. He later defeats the Green Goblin, but Gwen's loss changes him forever. From that day on, Peter becomes an even more determined hero, carrying the memory of Gwen with him in every battle.

Spider-Gwen (Alternate Universe)

In another Marvel universe called Earth-65, Gwen Stacy is the one bitten by the radioactive spider instead of Peter Parker. She becomes Spider-Woman, commonly known as Spider-Gwen. In this reality, Peter never becomes Spider-Man. Instead, after a tragic experiment, Peter dies, and Gwen dedicates her life to protecting others while carrying the guilt of losing her best friend.
