HTML5

The term "HTML5 words" most commonly refers to semantic tags, which are new structural words introduced in HTML5 to describe the meaning of content to browsers and search engines. It can also mean text formatting tags used to alter how specific words look

Semantic tag

Semantic tags in HTML are elements that clearly describe their meaning to both the browser and the developer. Unlike generic tags like

or which only act as empty content holders, semantic tags explain exactly what kind of content they contain.
