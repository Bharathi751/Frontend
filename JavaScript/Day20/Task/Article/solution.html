THE CHENNAI INDUSTRIAL INTELLIGENCE MODEL

Code for the physical world.

The next chapter of Chennai's technology economy should be measured not only by how many software services the city exports, but by how much intelligence its software adds to factories, vehicles, hospitals, electronics, ports and energy systems.

That means building a bridge between two economies that are often discussed separately: the digital economy and the physical economy.

What success looks like

A Chennai engineer should be able to move between a software team and a factory floor. A manufacturing company should be able to hire AI engineers who understand machines. A hospital should be able to build digital products with clinicians. A port operator should treat data as an operational asset.

The objective is not to preserve today's jobs. It is to make Chennai capable of creating the next generation of high-value jobs across both digital and physical systems.
