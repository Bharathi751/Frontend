01 — The uncoded powerhouse

Tamil Nadu is not merely an IT state. It is one of India's major manufacturing bases. Official industrial statistics place the state at roughly 15.43% of India's registered factories and 15.24% of persons engaged in registered manufacturing in ASI 2023–24.

That gives Chennai an unusual foundation: a city-region where ports, engineering talent, vehicle production, electronics, hospitals and universities already coexist. Remove software services from the equation and the industrial system remains — although its scale, investment and urban form would change.

02 — Automotive becomes the central axis

Chennai's automotive identity is one of the strongest alternatives to an IT-dominated growth story. Tamil Nadu's government investment portal cites more than 1,500 auto-component factories and around 35% of India's auto-component production.

In a counterfactual city, that ecosystem would likely pull more suppliers, toolmakers, testing laboratories, industrial design firms, training institutes and logistics providers into its orbit. The city would still be knowledge-intensive — but much of the knowledge would be embedded in physical production.

03 — Electronics: from assembly to intelligence

Tamil Nadu's electronics exports reached $7.37 billion during April 2023–January 2024, according to a state government release. The state said that figure represented 32.52% of India's ESDM exports for that period.

The next leap is not simply assembling more devices. It is moving up the stack: embedded systems, testing, industrial electronics, chip packaging, power electronics, sensors and design. That is where IT knowledge and industrial capability begin to merge.

04 — The maritime super-port

Chennai's geography creates another advantage: maritime connectivity. Chennai Port and Kamarajar Port can anchor a wider logistics ecosystem involving warehousing, customs, freight technology, cold chains, industrial parks and export manufacturing.

In the alternative city, logistics becomes not a supporting service but a core urban industry. The port is connected to factories; factories are connected to suppliers; suppliers are connected to research and testing. The city becomes a machine for moving physical value.

05 — Healthcare becomes an export industry

Chennai's hospital ecosystem provides another non-IT growth engine. In a stronger industrial counterfactual, healthcare expands beyond treatment into diagnostics, medical devices, rehabilitation technology, pharma manufacturing and clinical research.

That produces a different type of knowledge economy: doctors, engineers, device manufacturers, data specialists and researchers working around physical systems.
